01THE WHY

Curiosity over certainty.

The best way to understand a changing world is to stay curious about it. Zico by M2HB is a home for clear, considered writing on how things work—from the infrastructure that powers the web to the new tools reshaping what we can make.

No breathless predictions, no jargon for its own sake. Just useful explanations, honest questions, and ideas worth carrying with you.
