A concise introduction that gives readers a reason to keep going.

By M2HBOctober 8, 20265 min read

Start with a clear opening that introduces the question this story will explore. Give the reader enough context to understand why it matters.

Begin with the fundamentals

Use short paragraphs, informative subheadings, and concrete examples to make a complicated idea easier to follow. Link to trustworthy references when you make a claim that deserves further reading.

Make room for nuance. The most useful answer is often the one that explains its trade-offs.

Follow the idea through

Build on the explanation step by step. Keep the focus on what readers can learn, what remains uncertain, and what questions are worth asking next.

Close with a concise takeaway, then invite readers to continue exploring.
